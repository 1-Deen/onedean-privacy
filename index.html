Privacy Policy

دين واحد
Effective July 26, 2026 · Applies to OneDean for Android (com.onedean.app)

OneDean is a Quran, prayer-time, and daily-worship companion app. This page explains, in plain terms, what the app accesses on your device, where that information goes, and what stays local. There is no account system, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-tracking SDK anywhere in the app.

01

Location

If you grant location access, OneDean uses your device's coordinates to calculate accurate prayer times, point the Qibla compass toward the Kaaba, show local weather, and find nearby mosques.

Your coordinates are sent directly from your device to the prayer-time and weather providers listed in the table below — we do not run a server of our own that receives, stores, or logs your location. Location is requested only when a screen that needs it is open, and you can revoke access at any time in your device's Settings without losing access to the rest of the app.

02

Physical activity & step data

The step counter, exercise graphs, and star rewards read your daily step count through Android Health Connect, Google's on-device successor to Google Fit. Access is only read after you explicitly approve it on the Health Connect permission screen — OneDean never reads step data before that consent is given, and no Google account or sign-in is required.

If Health Connect isn't available or connected, OneDean falls back to your phone's own hardware step-counter sensor, then its motion sensor — both entirely on-device, with no external service involved either way.

Step history is stored locally on your device. If you use the "Export to Excel" feature, that file is written to your device's own storage; it is never uploaded to us. You can revoke step access at any time in Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions, or Android Settings → Apps → OneDean → Permissions.

03

Notifications

Notification and exact-alarm permissions are used solely to schedule local reminders on your device — prayer-time alerts and a daily hadith. These reminders are generated and delivered entirely on your device; no notification content or scheduling data is sent anywhere.

04

Data stored on your device

Settings, bookmarks, reading position, downloaded content, and step history are kept in local app storage. This never leaves your device except through an export action you initiate yourself. Uninstalling OneDean permanently deletes all of it.

06

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We don't share your data for advertising or marketing.
  • We don't operate a backend that stores your location or health data.
  • We don't require an account to use the Quran, prayer times, or reading features.
07

Children's privacy

OneDean is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

08

Account & data deletion

OneDean does not create or store its own user accounts, and no Google account or sign-in is required for any feature, including step tracking.

To remove OneDean's access to your step data:

  1. Open Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → OneDean, and turn off Steps
  2. Or open Android Settings → Apps → OneDean → Permissions, and revoke Physical activity

This immediately and permanently stops OneDean from reading your step data. We do not retain any copy of this data on our servers — there is no backend database of user data to delete, since all app data is stored locally on your device.

To delete all locally stored data (steps, bookmarks, settings, reading position): uninstall the app. This removes everything OneDean has stored on your device immediately, with no retention period, since nothing is kept elsewhere.

09

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of OneDean after a change means you accept the revised policy.
